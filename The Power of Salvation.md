# Correct Spiritual Posture in the Last Day Crisis

We have a non-logical reality.

**Posture 2 - A New Awareness and Celebration of the Power and Efficacy of salvation**

At the centre of all other postures is salvation.

> The simplicity of our life in Christ is the pathway through the complex events at the end of Tim.

The realities of our life must no longer be shaped by the natural but by the realities of the spirit realm.

## What is salvation?

John 3:1-2
An opening of the eyes and acceptance of the reality that man has sinned before God, man is broken and must enter into relationship with His Creator to effectively move forward.

There is life from above that shapes and forms us. Salvation allows us to see that life.

## What is the effect of Salvation?

* **Forgiveness of sins** - as if they never existed (Isaiah 1:18; Psalm 103:12; Colossians 2:13-14)
* **No condemnation** - A legal verdict, no penalty to pay; it is not a feeling (Romans 8:1)
* **Redemption** - a price paid to be released from bondage; you are now owned by another (1 Peter 1:18-19; Ephesians 1:7)
* **Transferred Kingdoms** - moved from the domain of darkness into the kingdom of light

--- 
* **Adoption as sons** - full legal sonship (Galations 4:5; Romans 8:15)
* **Reconciliation** - made peace with God (Romans 5:1-; 2 Corinthians 5: 18-19)
* **Declared to be citizens of Heavens** - (Ephesians 2:19; Philippians)
* **Declared Righteous** - (Romans 5:1; Romans 3:24)
